I am an interdisciplinary researcher in robotics and oceanography. My primary research interests are marine autonomy, optimal path planning, and adaptive sampling; on a high-level I am interested in how we can use autonomous systems to advance ocean observing.

Currently, I am an Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Michigan. I am co-mentored by Dr. Alan Papalia in the Robotic Exploration [RobEx](https://robex.engin.umich.edu/) Group and Dr. [Brian Arbic](https://arbic.earth.lsa.umich.edu/). Previously, I completed my Ph.D. at Scripps Institution of Oceanography (Applied Ocean Science) with Dr. Sophia Merrifield in the [CORDC](https://cordc.ucsd.edu/) group.
